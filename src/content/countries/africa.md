## Why Export from the UK to Africa?

The UK is one of the top sources for quality used vehicles destined for African markets. With strict MOT testing standards and well-maintained vehicles, UK exports offer excellent value and reliability for buyers across the continent.

## Our Export Process

1. **Vehicle Selection** — Browse our inventory or tell us what you need. We source vehicles that meet your exact specifications.
2. **Inspection & Documentation** — Every vehicle undergoes a thorough inspection. We prepare all export documentation including V5C, purchase invoice, and export certificate.
3. **Shipping & Logistics** — We handle RoRo (Roll-on Roll-off) or container shipping to major African ports.
4. **Customs Clearance Support** — Our team provides guidance on import duties, taxes, and local registration requirements.

## Popular Destinations

We regularly export vehicles to **Kenya, Tanzania, Nigeria, Ghana, South Africa, Uganda, Mozambique, Democratic Republic of Congo**, and many more African nations. Each country has specific import regulations, and we ensure full compliance with local requirements.

## Vehicle Types in Demand

African markets particularly favour durable, reliable vehicles suited to varied road conditions. The most popular categories include:

- **SUVs** — Toyota Land Cruiser, Nissan Patrol, Land Rover Discovery
- **Pickup Trucks** — Toyota Hilux, Ford Ranger, Nissan Navara
- **Saloons** — Toyota Corolla, Mercedes-Benz C-Class, BMW 3 Series
- **Commercial** — Toyota Dyna, Isuzu NQR, Mitsubishi Canter
