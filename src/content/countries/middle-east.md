## Why Export from the UK to the Middle East?

The Middle East market has a strong appetite for luxury and high-performance vehicles. UK-sourced vehicles offer excellent value compared to local dealer pricing, with well-documented service histories and lower mileage. Our export service connects Middle Eastern buyers with premium UK inventory.

## Our Export Process

1. **Vehicle Sourcing** — Access our curated inventory of luxury and premium vehicles, or commission us to find a specific model and specification.
2. **Quality Assurance** — Full mechanical and cosmetic inspection with detailed photographic report and service history verification.
3. **Shipping** — RoRo or containerised shipping via established routes through the Suez Canal to major Gulf ports.
4. **Compliance** — We ensure vehicles meet GCC specifications where required, including climate suitability and emissions compliance.

## Popular Destinations

We serve **United Arab Emirates, Oman, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Jordan**, and other Middle Eastern markets. Each country has specific import requirements, particularly around vehicle age limits and emissions standards.

## Vehicle Types in Demand

Middle Eastern buyers typically favour:

- **Luxury SUVs** — Range Rover, Mercedes GLE/GLS, BMW X5/X7, Audi Q7/Q8
- **Premium Sedans** — Mercedes S-Class, BMW 7 Series, Audi A8
- **Performance** — BMW M Series, Mercedes AMG, Audi RS models
- **Utility** — Toyota Land Cruiser, Nissan Patrol (for desert use)
