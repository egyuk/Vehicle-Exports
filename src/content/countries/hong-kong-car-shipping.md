## Weekly car shipping direct from the UK to Hong Kong

With dedicated container services, shared container services and Roll on Roll Off car shipping we offer the
most comprehensive way to ship your car to Hong Kong, with services and routes to suit your budget. We operate
one ship per week from the UK to Hong Kong, through the ports of Southampton and London.

### The three shipping systems

- **Roll on Roll Off (Ro-Ro).** Vehicles are driven on to the vessel and secured under deck. Sailings every 14
  days from Southampton.
- **20ft container.** You have the whole container, so you can also load spares or personal effects. Sailings
  weekly from London and Southampton.
- **Shared container.** Using the RRak system, four cars load into one 40ft container, which brings the
  shipping cost per car down.

Please make sure you are aware of current Hong Kong car import legislation and requirements before shipping
your car with us. For current rates, ask us for a quote.

## Guidelines for importation and registration of a motor vehicle

The Customs and Excise Department, the Environmental Protection Department and the Transport Department are
each involved separately in importing and registering a vehicle in Hong Kong. The guidelines below explain the
procedures. For a pure electric vehicle (PEV) or plug-in hybrid electric vehicle (PHEV), see the Transport
Department's separate guidelines for those.

### Step 1: Compliance with the exhaust and noise emissions standard

Get approval or exemption from the Environmental Protection Department **before** shipping your vehicle to
Hong Kong.

You must prove the vehicle complies with the Air Pollution Control (Vehicle Design Standards)(Emission)
Regulation and the Noise Control (Motor Vehicles) Regulation. Proof can be documents issued by the vehicle
manufacturer, or an emission testing report from an approved test laboratory. Submit the application form to
the Environmental Protection Department with all original supporting documents. Some categories of vehicle may
be exempt from the exhaust emission standard if they meet the exemption criteria, and you can apply to the
department for that exemption. Enquiries about approved test laboratories, emission standards and exemption
criteria: (852) 2877 0448 for exhaust emission, (852) 2411 9689 for noise emission, or fax (852) 2824 9361.

### Step 2: Importation

Vehicles imported into Hong Kong are not subject to customs tax, whatever their class, but an importer must
generally lodge an accurate and complete declaration with the Customs and Excise Department within 14 days of
importation.

Within 30 days of importation you must also submit an Import Return (Form CED336) enclosing a Declaration Form
(Form CED336A) to the Customs and Excise Department, or through the Motor Vehicles First Registration Tax
System website.

An importer who fails to file the import return within 30 days commits an offence, and is liable on conviction
to a fine of $500,000 and 12 months' imprisonment.

After you submit the Import Return you will receive a "Notification of Motor Vehicle Provisional Taxable Value"
from the Customs and Excise Department. Produce it when you first register the vehicle with the Transport
Department.

### Step 3: Vehicle examination

Before importing, make sure the vehicle meets the requirements for registration in Hong Kong and that you have
documentary evidence of compliance where it applies.

When the vehicle arrives, arrange examination at the Vehicle Examination Centre below. Bring the approval
letter from the Environmental Protection Department showing the car complies with the exhaust and noise
emission standards, and pay the examination fee.

| Address | Telephone |
| --- | --- |
| To Kwa Wan Vehicle Examination Centre, Long Yuet Street, Kowloon | 2333 3112 |

If you are exempt from the emission standard under Step 1, bring the exemption approval letter instead when
booking the examination. All imported vehicles with an internal combustion engine, including new cars imported
for personal use, may need an emission test during examination. A car that passes is issued a Certificate of
Roadworthiness by the Examination Centre.

### Step 4: Vehicle registration and licensing

To apply for first registration, submit a completed application form (Form TD22) to the Hong Kong Licensing
Office of the Transport Department (tel (852) 2804 2637, fax 2804 2599) with the supporting documents the form
prescribes. Once your documents are verified you pay the first registration tax and the registration fee, then
the vehicle licence fee and the levy for the Traffic Accident Victim Assistance Fund.

### Step 5: Calculation of first registration tax

First registration tax is the taxable value of the vehicle multiplied by the rate below.

| Class of motor vehicle | Rate of tax |
| --- | --- |
| I. Private cars — on the first $150,000 | 40% |
| I. Private cars — on the next $150,000 | 75% |
| I. Private cars — on the next $200,000 | 100% |
| I. Private cars — on the remainder | 115% |
| II. Motor cycles and motor tricycles | 35% |
| III(a). Goods vehicles, other than van-type light goods vehicles (LGV) | 15% |
| III(b). Van-type LGV not exceeding 1.9 tonnes — on the first $150,000 | 35% |
| III(b). Van-type LGV not exceeding 1.9 tonnes — on the next $150,000 | 65% |
| III(b). Van-type LGV not exceeding 1.9 tonnes — on the remainder | 85% |
| III(c). Van-type LGV exceeding 1.9 tonnes permitted gross vehicle weight | 17% |
| IV. Taxis, light buses, buses and special purpose vehicles | 3.7% |

An application to register and licence a **left-hand-drive vehicle will not be accepted** unless there are
special circumstances. UK stock is right-hand drive, which is one reason the route works.

## Notes for attention

- Applicants must produce proof of present address, both residential and correspondence. The Transport
  Department may suspend processing of a licensing application until address proof is produced.
- You can look up the current highest approved Published Retail Price (PRP) for a vehicle model using the
  search function in the Customs and Excise Department's Motor Vehicles First Registration Tax System,
  reachable from the Transport Department or Customs and Excise Department websites.
