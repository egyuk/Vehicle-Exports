## Why Export from the UK to the Caribbean?

The Caribbean has a strong tradition of importing right-hand drive vehicles from the UK and Japan. Many island nations drive on the left, making UK-spec vehicles a natural fit. Our competitive pricing and quality standards make us a preferred supplier for Caribbean buyers.

## Our Export Process

1. **Vehicle Selection** — Choose from our inventory or request a specific vehicle. We specialise in sourcing well-maintained, low-mileage UK vehicles.
2. **Inspection & Documentation** — Comprehensive pre-export inspection with full documentation package including V5C, bill of sale, and export certificate.
3. **Container Shipping** — Secure container shipping via Atlantic routes to major Caribbean ports.
4. **Import Guidance** — We provide detailed guidance on local import duties, age restrictions, and registration processes for each island.

## Popular Destinations

We export regularly to **Jamaica, Trinidad & Tobago, Barbados, Bahamas, Guyana, St Lucia, Grenada, Antigua & Barbuda**, and other Caribbean nations. Import regulations vary by island, and we ensure your vehicle meets all local requirements.

## Vehicle Types in Demand

Caribbean buyers typically look for:

- **Compact Cars** — Toyota Corolla, Honda Civic, Nissan Note
- **Compact SUVs** — Toyota RAV4, Nissan Qashqai, Honda CR-V
- **Luxury Sedans** — BMW 3 Series, Mercedes-Benz C-Class, Audi A4
- **Minibuses** — Toyota HiAce, Nissan Caravan (for commercial transport)
