## Why Export from the UK to Europe?

Despite Brexit, the UK remains one of the top sources for quality vehicles destined for European markets. With competitive pricing and a wide selection of premium brands, UK exports offer exceptional value for buyers across the continent.

## Our Export Process

1. **Vehicle Selection** — Browse our inventory or tell us what you need. We source vehicles that meet your exact specifications.
2. **Inspection & Documentation** — Every vehicle undergoes a thorough inspection. We prepare all export documentation including V5C, purchase invoice, and export certificate.
3. **Shipping & Logistics** — We handle RoRo (Roll-on Roll-off) or container shipping to major European ports.
4. **Customs Clearance Support** — Our team provides guidance on post-Brexit import duties, taxes, and local registration requirements.

## Popular Destinations

We regularly export vehicles to **Malta, Cyprus, Greece, Spain, Portugal, Italy, Netherlands, Germany**, and many more European nations. Each country has specific import regulations, and we ensure full compliance with local requirements.

## Vehicle Types in Demand

European markets particularly favour premium and luxury vehicles. The most popular categories include:

- **SUVs** — Range Rover Sport, BMW X5, Audi Q7, Porsche Cayenne
- **Luxury Saloons** — Mercedes-Benz E-Class, BMW 5 Series, Audi A6
- **Sports Cars** — Porsche 911, BMW M4, Mercedes-AMG
- **Compact Premium** — BMW 3 Series, Audi A4, Mercedes C-Class
