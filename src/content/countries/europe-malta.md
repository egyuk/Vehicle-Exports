## Why Ship Vehicles from the UK to Malta?

Malta is one of the most popular European destinations for UK vehicle exports. With a strong demand for right-hand drive vehicles and competitive pricing from the UK market, Maltese buyers benefit greatly from importing quality used cars.

## Import Requirements for Malta

Malta has specific import regulations that must be followed:

- **Registration Tax** — Calculated based on CO2 emissions and vehicle value
- **VAT** — 18% VAT applies on the vehicle value plus shipping costs
- **Customs Duty** — Post-Brexit, a customs duty may apply depending on vehicle origin
- **VRT Inspection** — Vehicle must pass a roadworthiness test upon arrival

## Popular Vehicle Types in Malta

Due to Malta's compact roads and limited parking, smaller and mid-size vehicles are highly popular:

- **Compact Cars** — Toyota Yaris, VW Polo, Ford Fiesta
- **Saloons** — BMW 3 Series, Mercedes C-Class, Audi A4
- **Small SUVs** — Nissan Qashqai, Toyota RAV4, BMW X1
- **Electric/Hybrid** — Growing demand for eco-friendly vehicles with tax incentives
