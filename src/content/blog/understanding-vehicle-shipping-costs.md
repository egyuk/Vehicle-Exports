## The True Cost of Shipping a Vehicle

One of the most common questions we receive is: "How much will shipping cost?" The answer depends on several factors, but we believe in full transparency. Here's a detailed breakdown.

## Freight Costs

Shipping rates vary by destination and method:

### RoRo Shipping (Roll-on Roll-off)
- **West Africa (Lagos, Tema):** $800 – $1,200
- **East Africa (Mombasa, Dar es Salaam):** $1,000 – $1,500
- **Caribbean (Kingston, Port of Spain):** $1,200 – $1,800
- **Middle East (Jeddah, Dubai):** $600 – $1,000

### Container Shipping (20ft)
- **West Africa:** $1,500 – $2,500
- **East Africa:** $1,800 – $2,800
- **Caribbean:** $2,000 – $3,000
- **Middle East:** $1,200 – $1,800

Container shipping costs more but allows you to ship personal items alongside the vehicle and offers better protection from the elements.

## Insurance

Marine insurance typically costs 1.5% to 2.5% of the vehicle's declared value. We strongly recommend full coverage — the small premium is worth the peace of mind.

## Import Duties and Taxes

This is often the largest cost after the vehicle itself. Rates vary dramatically:

| Destination | Duty Rate | VAT/Tax | Total Estimate |
|------------|-----------|---------|----------------|
| Kenya | 25% | 16% VAT | ~45% of CIF value |
| Nigeria | 20% | 7.5% VAT | ~30% of CIF value |
| Jamaica | 30-40% | 21.5% GCT | ~55% of CIF value |
| UAE | 5% | 5% VAT | ~10% of CIF value |

CIF = Cost + Insurance + Freight (the total landed value at the destination port).

## Additional Costs

Don't forget these commonly overlooked expenses:

- **Port handling fees:** $100 – $300
- **Clearing agent fees:** $150 – $400
- **Pre-shipment inspection:** $100 – $250
- **Local registration:** Varies by country
- **Inland transport (port to door):** $50 – $500

## How to Save Money

1. **Book early** — shipping rates fluctuate with demand
2. **Choose RoRo over container** if you don't need the extra protection
3. **Consolidate shipments** — shipping multiple vehicles in one container reduces per-unit cost
4. **Work with experienced exporters** — we negotiate bulk rates with shipping lines
