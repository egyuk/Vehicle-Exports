## Chinese Car Exports Surge as EV Demand Grows

According to industry data reported by the China Association of Automobile Manufacturers, Chinese passenger car exports rose sharply in May, climbing to roughly 809,000 vehicles — a year-on-year increase of around 73%, and up from about 796,000 units in April.

## New Energy Vehicles Lead the Growth

Exports of new energy vehicles — including battery electric and plug-in hybrid models — more than doubled compared with the same month last year, reaching an estimated 435,000 units and accounting for over half of all exports for the month.

Higher fuel prices globally have been pushing more buyers toward electric and hybrid alternatives, and Chinese manufacturers such as BYD have used this shift to accelerate their expansion into markets across Latin America, Asia, and Europe, partly to offset softer demand at home as domestic EV incentives have been scaled back.

## What This Means for Vehicle Exporters

For international buyers and importers, the growing supply of competitively priced EVs and hybrids is reshaping export markets that were traditionally dominated by used petrol and diesel vehicles. It's a trend worth watching for anyone sourcing vehicles for export, particularly to regions where fuel costs and import incentives are shifting demand toward electrified models.
