## Africa's Most Wanted Vehicles

Africa remains one of the largest markets for UK vehicle exports, with demand focused on durability, reliability, and value. Here are the top five vehicles driving exports this year.

## 1. Toyota Hilux

The Hilux is virtually indestructible — and African buyers know it. Known for handling rough terrain, extreme heat, and heavy loads, the Hilux is the go-to workhorse across the continent. The 2.4-litre diesel variant is especially popular for its fuel efficiency and parts availability.

**Price range (UK):** $18,000 – $32,000
**Best for:** East Africa, West Africa, Southern Africa

## 2. Toyota Land Cruiser

For those who need a full-size SUV that can go anywhere, the Land Cruiser has no equal. Whether it's the Prado or the full-size 300 series, these vehicles hold their value incredibly well in African markets.

**Price range (UK):** $35,000 – $65,000
**Best for:** Kenya, Tanzania, Nigeria, DRC

## 3. Nissan Patrol

A favourite in North Africa and the Middle East, the Patrol is now gaining serious traction in Sub-Saharan Africa as well. Its V8 engine and spacious cabin make it a premium choice.

**Price range (UK):** $28,000 – $50,000
**Best for:** North Africa, Nigeria, Sudan

## 4. Land Rover Defender

The new Defender has won over fans who loved the original's ruggedness but wanted modern comfort. It's becoming a popular choice for NGOs, safari operators, and private buyers across Africa.

**Price range (UK):** $40,000 – $70,000
**Best for:** East Africa, safari operations

## 5. Ford Ranger

The Ranger combines affordability with capability. Its 2.0-litre EcoBlue diesel is refined yet powerful, and the double-cab configuration makes it practical for both work and family use.

**Price range (UK):** $22,000 – $38,000
**Best for:** Southern Africa, West Africa

## Key Takeaways

When choosing a vehicle for export to Africa, prioritise:

- **Diesel engines** — better fuel economy and readily available fuel
- **Automatic and manual options** — manual is still preferred in many markets
- **Parts availability** — Toyota and Nissan parts networks are the strongest
- **Age restrictions** — check your destination country's import age limits
