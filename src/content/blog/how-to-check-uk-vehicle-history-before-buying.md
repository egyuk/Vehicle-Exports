## Why Vehicle History Matters

Buying a vehicle for export is a significant investment. Whether you're spending $15,000 or $60,000, you need confidence that the vehicle is exactly what it claims to be. Here's how to verify a UK vehicle's history.

## 1. Check the MOT History (Free)

The UK government provides free MOT history checks at gov.uk. Enter the vehicle's registration number to see:

- Every MOT test date and result (pass/fail)
- Recorded mileage at each test
- Advisory notes and failure reasons
- Test expiry dates

This is the single most valuable free check you can do. Consistent mileage readings across MOT tests confirm the odometer hasn't been tampered with.

## 2. HPI Check / Vehicle History Report

For a more thorough investigation, run an HPI or similar check. These paid services reveal:

- **Outstanding finance** — Is there money still owed on the vehicle?
- **Insurance write-off status** — Has the vehicle been declared a total loss?
- **Stolen vehicle check** — Is it on the Police National Computer?
- **Number plate changes** — Has the registration been swapped?
- **Import/export status** — Was it previously imported?

We run HPI checks on every vehicle in our inventory as standard.

## 3. Service History

A full service history from authorised dealers is the gold standard. It confirms:

- Regular maintenance was performed
- Genuine parts were used
- Any recalls were addressed
- The vehicle was cared for by professionals

Vehicles with full service history command higher prices but offer significantly better reliability.

## 4. V5C Registration Document

The V5C (logbook) contains essential information:

- Registered keeper details
- Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)
- Engine number
- Date of first registration
- Colour and body type

Always verify that the VIN on the V5C matches the VIN stamped on the vehicle itself.

## 5. Physical Inspection

Nothing replaces a thorough physical inspection. Check for:

- Paint inconsistencies (indicating previous repairs)
- Panel gaps (signs of accident damage)
- Tyre condition and matching brands
- Interior wear consistent with stated mileage
- Engine bay cleanliness and fluid levels
