## Vehicle Details

The 2024 Porsche 911 GT3 RS is the most track-focused road-legal 911 ever built. Its naturally aspirated 4.0-litre flat-six engine revs to 9,000rpm, delivering 518bhp of pure, visceral power through Porsche's lightning-fast PDK dual-clutch transmission.

### Aerodynamics

The centrepiece is the DRS (Drag Reduction System) rear wing — a first for any production Porsche. Active aerodynamic elements across the body generate up to 409kg of downforce at 177mph, providing race-car levels of grip on road and track alike.

### Track Performance

Fitted with the Weissach Package, this GT3 RS features carbon fibre bodywork, magnesium wheels, PCCB ceramic composite brakes, and a lightweight roll cage. Every component is engineered to shed weight and sharpen response.
