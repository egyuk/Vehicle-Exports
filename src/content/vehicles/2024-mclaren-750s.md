## Vehicle Details

The 2024 McLaren 750S represents the pinnacle of McLaren's Super Series. Powered by a 4.0-litre twin-turbocharged V8 producing 740bhp, this supercar delivers breathtaking performance with a 0-62mph time of just 2.8 seconds.

### Performance

Built around McLaren's carbon fibre Monocage central structure, the 750S weighs just 1,277kg dry — making it one of the lightest supercars in its class. The active rear wing and optimised aerodynamics generate 30% more downforce than the 720S it replaces.

### Interior

The cabin features lightweight Alcantara and leather trim, a folding driver display that minimises distractions in track mode, and McLaren's latest infotainment system with satellite navigation and Bowers & Wilkins premium audio.
