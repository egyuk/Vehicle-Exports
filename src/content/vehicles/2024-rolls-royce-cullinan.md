## Vehicle Details

The 2024 Rolls-Royce Cullinan Black Badge is the most commanding luxury SUV in existence. In exclusive Black Badge specification, this Cullinan features enhanced power from its 6.75-litre twin-turbocharged V12 engine, producing 592bhp and 900Nm of torque for effortless performance.

### Black Badge

The Black Badge treatment transforms the Cullinan with darkened chrome elements, a unique Spirit of Ecstasy, 22-inch forged alloy wheels, and bespoke interior touches that set it apart from the standard model. Every surface has been considered and refined.

### Interior

The cabin is a masterclass in bespoke luxury. The Starlight Headliner recreates a constellation of fibre-optic stars, while the Rear Theatre Configuration provides individual screens, fold-out tables, and a refrigerator compartment. Lambswool floor mats and hand-stitched leather complete the experience.

### Export Ready

Supplied with complete Rolls-Royce documentation, full manufacturer warranty, and all export paperwork prepared. White-glove enclosed shipping available worldwide.
