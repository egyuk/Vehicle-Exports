## Vehicle Details

The 2024 Mercedes-AMG G 63 combines iconic G-Class design with hand-crafted AMG performance. The 4.0-litre V8 Biturbo engine delivers 577bhp and 850Nm of torque, propelling this luxury SUV from 0-62mph in just 4.5 seconds.

### Performance

The AMG-enhanced 9-speed automatic transmission and permanent all-wheel drive system with three differential locks ensure supreme capability on any terrain. AMG RIDE CONTROL suspension adapts to driving conditions, offering everything from comfort cruising to dynamic handling.

### Luxury Interior

Hand-finished with the finest materials, the cabin features Nappa leather upholstery, AMG carbon fibre trim, heated and ventilated front seats, and the Burmester surround sound system delivering crystal-clear audio through 16 speakers.
